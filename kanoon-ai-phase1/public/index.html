OUR PURPOSE

Legal information, with people in mind.

KANOON AI is a project concept designed to make legal information easier to explore and understand, with an India-first focus.

What this platform aims to do

Help people find relevant public legal resources, organise questions, understand common terminology and prepare for conversations with qualified legal professionals.

What it does not do

It does not replace an advocate, create an advocate-client relationship, guarantee outcomes, or determine the correct legal strategy for a particular case.

Our source-first approach

Future AI answers should point to official legislation, court websites and legal-services resources wherever possible, and state clearly when information has not been verified.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Legal disclaimer.

Read this before using any information on the platform.

General information only

Content displayed in this first milestone is general information and sample interface content. It is not legal advice, a legal opinion, or a substitute for advice from a qualified advocate familiar with your facts and jurisdiction.

No guarantee of accuracy or completeness

Laws and procedures change. Demo content may be incomplete and has not been verified for your specific situation. Independently confirm current legislation, amendments, rules, deadlines and court procedures through authoritative sources.

Urgent matters

If a deadline, arrest, threat, domestic violence, cybercrime, or other urgent issue is involved, contact the appropriate authorities or a qualified legal professional promptly.

No confidential uploads in demo mode

Do not submit confidential, identifying, financial or sensitive personal information. The demo tools do not provide secure document processing or user-specific storage.

YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy by design.

This first milestone is a static demo. It does not connect to a database or an AI provider.

What this demo does

Interactive examples run in your browser. This project code does not send the text you type in the demo chat, document explainer or draft generator to a project backend.

Third-party services

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may contact Google's servers to fetch them. You can remove the font imports from public/index.html if you prefer system fonts only.

Future versions

Before adding accounts, AI APIs, analytics or uploads, publish a complete privacy notice explaining what is collected, why it is used, retention, processors, deletion options and contact details. Secure all private records with access controls.
